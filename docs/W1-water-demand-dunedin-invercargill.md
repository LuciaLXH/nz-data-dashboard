# NZ Municipal Water Supply Performance Data — Dunedin & Invercargill

**Research compiled: August 2026. All figures are exact as published in the cited sources — none derived/fabricated unless explicitly marked "derived".**

**Headline caveat:** Water New Zealand's National Performance Review (NPR) per-supplier datasets (the source of "water supplied" in litres/person/day and metering %) are login-gated at waternz.org.nz, and the final NPR (2021/22) archived document is a definitions/mapping document, not data tables. The NPR's successor is Taumata Arowai's **Network Environmental Performance Report (NEPR)**, which reports per-**connection** (not per-capita) metrics for every council. Per-capita consumption is reported by each council in its own documents. Both are given below.

### 2.3 Metering coverage

- **0% of domestic connections metered.** DCC 9-Year Plan 2025-34 (Water efficiency section): "At present, domestic water connections in Dunedin are unmetered; each property is charged a fixed rate for water supply regardless of how much water they use." The DCC expects to begin community discussion on domestic metering and user-pays charging around the next 10 Year Plan (2027/37); smart-metered "tobies" renewal is a selected option (Years 6-30+, $41M/$80M).
- Dunedin City Council does **not** appear in the Taumata Arowai NEPR 2024/25 "Table 5: Organisations with water meters installed on at least 90% of residential network connections" → residential metering <90%.

### 2.4 Population served & connections

- Registered population by supply (DCC Servicing Assessment Report, Table 4, p.23, as at 30 June 2024): Dunedin City (DUN001) **112,515**; Outram (OUT001) **750**; Waikouaiti (WAI015) **1,642**; West Taieri (WES002) **450** (plus Dunedin Airport). **Total registered: 115,357.**
- **48,033 total water connections as at 30 June 2024** (DCC WSDP, "Serviced areas (by reticulated network)" table): Dunedin 47,554; Waikouaiti 1,105; Outram 434; West Taieri 155 (non-residential scheme). *(Note: the sum of the four schemes is 49,248; the WSDP states the total as 48,033 — the document itself is internally inconsistent; quoted as published.)*
- Projected serviced population (water supply) FY2024/25: **116,058** (DCC WSDP); projected residential water connections FY2024/25: 46,213.
- DCC services approximately 86% of the area's population for water (DCC WSDP).

## 5. Caveats & methodology notes

1. **NPR data access:** Water New Zealand's NPR per-supplier data (water supplied L/p/d, metering %, connections) is behind a member login. The [final NPR (2021/22) document](https://www.waternz.org.nz/Attachment?Action=Download&Attachment_id=5261) (archived [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20230928180028/https://www.waternz.org.nz/Attachment?Action=Download&Attachment_id=5261)) is a definitions/NEPM-mapping document, not the data tables. Water NZ explicitly noted the NPR ended: "This is the final review by Water New Zealand. From 2023 onwards reporting is required by regulation under Taumata Arowai through the Network Environmental Performance Measures."
2. **Per-connection vs per-capita:** Taumata Arowai NEPR metrics are per-connection (L/connection/day). Per-capita (L/person/day) values come from council documents and are *consumption* figures, not supply.
3. **Dunedin consumption confidence:** DCC WSDP: "Data confidence regarding drinking water consumption is generally low / uncertain. This is due to the small number of domestic meters used to calculate domestic consumption" (Dunedin is essentially unmetered).
4. **ICC consumption assessment:** ICC 3 Waters AMP: results for 2017/18–2021/22 "are suspect"; for 2022/23 "a lesser assessment for residential property consumption was applied" (551 vs 610 L/property/day). Since 2020/21 the measure covers domestic consumption only.
5. **Loss % methodologies differ:** DCC "real water loss %" uses non-revenue-water ÷ production; ICC uses "Water NZ Water Loss Guidelines" methodology; Taumata CARL is L/connection/day. Figures are not directly comparable across these definitions.
6. **Year labeling:** DCC WSDP table columns are Target | Actual 2024/25 | Actual 2023/24 (verified); the DCC 9YP 2025-34 quotes only "Actual 2023-24" (276 L/day; 15%), consistent with the WSDP 2023/24 column.
7. **Document dates:** DCC WSDP submitted 25 August 2025; DCC 9-Year Plan 2025-34 (adopted 2025); ICC 3 Waters AMP 2024 (Draft, "3Ws Addendum"); ICC LTP 2024-34 (adopted 2024); ICC Performance Report Q3 2025 (report date 20 May 2025); Taumata NEPR 2023/24 (published 2025); NEPR 2024/25 (published 2026).
8. **Taumata Arowai caution:** "comparisons between councils should be treated with care because networks differ in age, size, geography and the quality of reporting. Improved metering and better data collection can also affect reported results over time" (NEPR 2024/25; also quoted in Lizard News 11 Aug 2026).
