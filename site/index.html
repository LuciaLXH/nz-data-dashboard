Share of consented water volume by use, per region (LAWA, CC BY 4.0). A consent authorises a take — it is not actual use. How this was built & why I don’t claim causation

All pipeline code, SQL and tests are open source: github.com/LuciaLXH/nz-data-dashboard (GitHub Actions refreshes the data; the site is rebuilt and deployed from main).

Pipeline & SQL (click to expand)

Stats NZ population (2018–2025) × Taumata Arowai NEPR 2024/25 unit-level network data (all registered supplies in the 6 regions) → per-region implied daily demand, leakage and 2030 projection. All business logic lives in sql/, executed by DuckDB; the static site reads only data/processed/*.json.

  • sql/01 — population YoY + 5-yr CAGR (Stats NZ SDMX API, CC BY 4.0)
  • sql/02 — per-capita supply, leakage, CARL, ILI, implied demand + 2030 projection (NEPR 2024/25, CC BY 3.0 NZ)
  • sql/03 — river-flow same-week percentile per monitored site (council Hilltop servers)
Why I don’t claim causation
  • Ecological fallacy — council-level numbers say nothing about catchment-level mechanism.
  • n = 6 — no correlation across this few units is robust; one large council can flip a sign.
  • Confounding — land use (dairy intensity, irrigation) drives NZ river water quality far more than urban population. Section B in ANALYSIS.md sets out the test; analysis pending.
  • Self-reported — NEPR/NPR figures are council-reported; metering coverage varies.
  • Consents ≠ use — the consent chart shows authorised volume, not actual takes.
Flow data caveats (read before citing the circles)
  • Curated sample, not the full network — 11 sites from 2 of the 6 councils (HBRC live telemetry; ORC historical). Only these councils publish open flow time series; Canterbury/Southland/Auckland public endpoints expose metadata only, Waikato’s is down.
  • ORC is historical — the public server froze at 2021-04-23; circles show the last observation vs same-week history at the end of the public record, not current conditions.
  • Coordinates: 5 sites verified from LAWA datasets; 3 approximate (riverapp.net / OpenStreetMap); 3 have no public coordinates and appear in the table only.
